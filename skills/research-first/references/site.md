# Questions for a site

- Static, server-rendered or app-like: which fits the content and the update rate? Check the framework's own rendering documentation.
- Hosting and cost: the free tier limits and the exit path, from the host's official pricing page.
- Accessibility and performance targets: WCAG level from W3C, Core Web Vitals thresholds from web.dev.
- SEO and metadata basics from the search engines' own documentation.
- Privacy and cookies (GDPR when a form or analytics collects data), spam protection and domain.
- Existing open-source templates or sites of the same kind: licence, last commit, what to borrow.
