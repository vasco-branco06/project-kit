# Type rules: research

- Keep a source log in `source-log.md`: full citation, where found, access date, what it says, relevance, own comment.
- No claim without a source that was opened and read. If sources conflict, show both and say which you trust and why.
- Prefer primary and official sources.
- Raw inputs stay untouched in `inputs/`; derived material goes in `studies/` or `work/`.
